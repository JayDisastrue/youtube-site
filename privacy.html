Comms Off Studio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Comms Off Studio ("the app") is a private tool that runs on its owner's computer. It edits stream clips and publishes them to accounts owned by Comms Off. This policy explains what account data the app uses and what it does with it.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, and read basic channel statistics. By using the app's YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • Sign-in tokens for the YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts you connect, so the app can post on your behalf.
  • Channel and account statistics: follower or subscriber count, video count, views, and the stats of recent uploads.
  • The videos, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails the app creates and uploads.

The app does not access your contacts, messages, comments, payment details or any other account data.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload clips to the connected accounts and to show their statistics in the app's dashboard. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage

Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only on the computer running the app. They are never sent to a server run by Comms Off, because there is none. Data goes only to the platform it belongs to (Google/YouTube, TikTok or Meta/Instagram) through their official APIs.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any user data to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are cached for up to 10 minutes. Sign-in tokens are kept until the account is removed from the app, which deletes its token. You can also revoke the app's access at any time:

  • Google/YouTube: Google security settings, Third-party apps
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services permissions
  • Instagram: Settings, Website permissions, Apps and websites

Once access is revoked, the app can no longer read or post to that account. To ask for any remaining data to be deleted, use the contact address below.

Cookies

The app and this website do not use cookies or tracking.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
